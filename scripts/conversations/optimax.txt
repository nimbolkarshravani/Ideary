Assistant: Structured-Objective Chat: v1 Concept Doc

1. Problem statement
Current AI chat interfaces are reactive and opaque. The user carries the entire cognitive load of defining the goal, steering the conversation, and evaluating progress, while the AI silently forms its own assumptions about what's being solved. When those assumptions diverge from the user's actual intent, neither party sees it until the conversation has already drifted. The structure of the problem (objective, constraints, open questions) is never made visible, even though it's the thing that determines whether the conversation succeeds.

2. Who has this problem
Independent thinkers and solo builders who use AI as a thinking partner, not a code generator. Specifically, people who come to chat with half-formed ideas, ideate across long sessions, and have repeatedly experienced the frustration of a conversation going in circles or landing on the wrong answer because the AI was solving a slightly different problem the whole time. Founders mid-ideation, analysts working through ambiguous questions, writers structuring arguments, anyone whose work involves moving from vague to concrete.

3. Why current solutions fail
ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini are all built around the same turn-taking pattern: user prompts, AI responds, AI waits. They optimize for response quality per turn, not for clarity of the underlying problem across turns. None of them surface what they think the objective is, which constraints they've inferred, or what's still undefined. The user has to hold all of that in their head and constantly check whether the AI is still aligned. This
Assistant: works for short transactional queries and breaks down on anything that requires sustained collaboration.

4. The solution in user-visible terms
A chat interface with two panes. The left is a normal chat. The right is a live panel with four fields:
* Objective
* Constraints
* Open questions
* Assumptions

The panel updates automatically as the conversation evolves. The user can edit any field directly to correct the AI's interpretation. An optional toggle enables proactive mode, in which the AI pushes the conversation forward when fields are empty or contradictory, instead of waiting passively. The panel is always visible, the proactivity is opt-in.

5. v1 success criteria
* I use it daily for two weeks in place of regular chat AI for ideation work.
* The panel correctly captures the objective without manual editing in at least 70 percent of conversations.
* At least 3 of 5 test users report that the panel changed how they thought about their problem, not just how they interacted with the AI.
* At least one test user asks to keep using it after the test period ends.
